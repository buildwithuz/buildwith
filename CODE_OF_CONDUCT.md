# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(translations are listed on that site).

**In short:** be respectful, welcome newcomers, criticise ideas not people, no harassment, no discrimination.

It applies in the repository, Discussions, the Telegram group, and on buildwith.uz itself.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to **conduct@buildwith.uz** or to any maintainer on Telegram. Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers will respond within 72 hours and follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban).
