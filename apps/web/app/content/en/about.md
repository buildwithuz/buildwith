# About buildwith.uz

buildwith.uz is a home for Uzbek engineers: a place to show what you are building, ask questions, write about what you learned and find the people who do the same work.

## Why it exists

Uzbek engineers are scattered across Telegram channels, foreign forums and social networks built to keep you scrolling. Good answers sink in chat history, and good work is hard to find a year later. We wanted one calm, searchable place that belongs to the community and is written in our languages.

## What makes it different

- **Open source.** All of the code is public under the [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/buildwithuz/buildwith) licence. Anyone can read how it works, suggest a change or run their own copy.
- **One server in Uzbekistan.** The site and its data live on a single server in Uzbekistan. No paid cloud services, no external CDNs.
- **No ads, no trackers.** There are no third-party analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels. We do not sell or share your data.
- **No algorithmic feed.** _Following_ shows posts from the people and tags you follow, in time order. The front page is ranked by votes and age with a public formula, not by what keeps you on the site longest.
- **Your languages.** The interface is in Uzbek, Russian and English, and you can read everything in Latin or Cyrillic script. Code, links and usernames are never transliterated.

## Four surfaces, one account

### Posts

Links and text posts ranked by the community, in the spirit of Hacker News. Two kinds have their own pages. **Savol** is for questions: answers are voted on, and the best one rises to the top. **Koʻrsat** is for showing something you built so others can try it and give feedback.

### Notes

Short posts about what you are working on right now: a bug you just fixed, a library you found, a small win. Up to 500 characters, with images or a code snippet. Notes stay in Notes and in Following; the most liked ones appear in a small box on the front page.

### Articles

Longer write-ups for when a note is not enough. The editor works in Markdown with a live preview, keeps drafts and versions, and the reading page is set in a serif typeface at a comfortable width. You can publish now or schedule for later, group articles into a series and point to the original if you first published elsewhere.

### Profiles

Your page at buildwith.uz/@you: a README, your stack, pinned GitHub repositories, a year of activity and an “Open to work” badge if you want one. It is meant to be the link you put on your CV.

## Moderation in the open

A small team of volunteer moderators keeps the site useful. They are listed by name, and every action they take, from hiding a post to banning an account, is recorded in the public [moderation log](/modlog). The rules everyone follows are in the [community guidelines](/guidelines).

## Your data is yours

We keep as little personal data as we can; the [privacy policy](/privacy) explains exactly what. In [settings](/settings) you can download everything you have posted as a ZIP file or delete your account at any time.

## How to contribute

buildwith.uz is built by its users. The code lives at [github.com/buildwithuz/buildwith](https://github.com/buildwithuz/buildwith).

- **Report a bug or suggest an idea** in the issues or discussions.
- **Send a pull request.** Issues labelled `good first issue` are a good place to start, and the README explains how to run the whole stack on your laptop.
- **Help with translations.** All interface text lives in one file per language; fixes to Uzbek, Russian and English wording are very welcome.
- **Report security problems privately** through GitHub security advisories on the repository, not in a public issue.

If you are not a programmer, you can still help: write a good article, answer a Savol, or invite a colleague who would enjoy it here.
