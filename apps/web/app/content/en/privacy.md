# Privacy policy

We collect as little about you as the site needs to work, keep it in Uzbekistan, and never sell it or show it to advertisers. This page explains exactly what we store and why.

> **Draft.** This policy will be reviewed by a lawyer before launch and may change. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

## What we store

### Your profile

What you enter yourself: your name, username, pronouns, bio, role and company, location, links, stack, README, and the avatar and banner you upload. All of it is public on your profile, and you can change or remove it at any time in [settings](/settings).

### How you sign in

We don't use passwords. When you sign in with GitHub, Google or Telegram, we store that provider's account id so we can recognise you next time. With GitHub we also keep your public username to show the verified badge and your pinned repositories.

We **don't store your email address**. The only exception is if you choose to verify a company email for the “Company verified” badge: we send one confirmation message and then keep **only the domain** (for example, `uzum.uz`), not the address.

### What you post and do

Your posts, notes, articles, comments and their edit history; your votes, likes, reposts and bookmarks; who and which tags you follow; who you mute or block, and the words you mute. Votes and mutes are private. Everything you post is public unless a moderator hides it.

### Preferences

Your language, script (Latin or Cyrillic) and theme, so the site looks the same on every device.

### Cookies

We use only two cookies, and both are strictly necessary: a **session cookie** that keeps you signed in, and a **CSRF cookie** that protects your account from forged requests. If you are not signed in, we also remember your language, script and theme in small cookies. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

## What we don't do

- No third-party trackers, analytics or tracking pixels.
- No advertising, and no selling or sharing your data with anyone.
- No external CDNs or embedded third-party scripts: fonts, images and code are served from our own server. Embeds such as YouTube or GitHub gists are shown as plain links.
- Every image you upload is re-encoded, and its metadata — including EXIF data and GPS location — is removed before it is stored.

## Server logs

Like any web server, ours records requests (time, page, IP address) to protect the site from attacks and abuse. These logs are rotated automatically: only the most recent ones are kept (about 100 MB), and older entries are overwritten, usually within days. We never link them to your profile, and the application's own logs contain no IP addresses.

## Where your data lives

All data is stored on one server located in the Republic of Uzbekistan, in line with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan “On Personal Data”. Backups are made daily, kept for 14 days, and stored in Uzbekistan as well.

## Telegram notifications

If you turn on Telegram notifications, we store your Telegram chat id so the bot can send you messages. Send `/stop` to the bot and the chat id is deleted; send `/start` to link it again.

## Your rights

- **See and export everything.** In [settings](/settings) you can download all your data — profile, posts, notes, articles and comments — as a ZIP file with JSON and Markdown.
- **Correct it.** You can edit your profile at any time; posts can be edited within their edit window.
- **Delete your account.** Deleting your account erases your personal data for good. Your posts and comments stay so that discussions keep making sense, but they are shown as “[deleted]” and are no longer linked to you.

## Who can see what

Your profile and everything you post are public and can be read by anyone, including through our API and RSS feeds. Moderators can see reports and hidden content. Nobody at buildwith.uz reads your votes or mutes except when investigating abuse such as vote manipulation.
