# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public issue.** Report privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/buildwithuz/buildwith/security/advisories/new),
or email **security@buildwith.uz**.

Include steps to reproduce, impact, and (if you have one) a suggested fix.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within **72 hours**.
- A fix or plan within **14 days** for high-severity issues.
- Credit in the release notes and advisory, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope

The code in this repository and the production site at buildwith.uz.
Out of scope: denial of service by traffic volume, social engineering, issues in third-party services.

Please test only against your own local instance, not other users' accounts.
